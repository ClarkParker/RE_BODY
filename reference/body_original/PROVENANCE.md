# Provenance — BODY original

These two files are the unmodified original plugin "BODY" as supplied by the repository owner
(uploaded 2026-10-15, files `dsp.cmajor` 54,694 bytes and `index.js` 38,442 bytes).
They are the baseline that RE·BODY extends. Do not edit them; diff against them.

Missing from the upload: the `.cmajorpatch` manifest of the original.

## Origin (owner's information, 2026-10-15)
- Author: Artists in DSP. BODY and HONE are "first experiments" inspired by two Reddit ideas
  (credits in the announcement: nizzernammer and Electus93). Video: "Reddit Asked for an Obosaxolele. So I Tried."
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S1xCaFG2uiw (patch links in the video description).
- Distribution: the open source was given to a closed developer group with the invitation
  "curious what you do with it". No deadline, no formal submission rules.
- Consequence for RE·BODY: credit Artists in DSP as the original author of BODY in the plugin
  description and README; keep the original's identity recognisable.
